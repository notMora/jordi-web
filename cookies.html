verified_user Do I have to accept cookies?

No. Analytics cookies are only set if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner.

If you reject them, or simply ignore the banner, no cookies are set and the site works exactly the same. Apart from that, the site only stores the language you choose and your cookie choice. That is technical storage that identifies no one, so under European rules (Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC) and Swiss rules (Art. 45c of the Telecommunications Act) it does not need your consent — you only need to be informed, which is what this page does.

01 // What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar browser technology for saving small settings on your device; unlike cookies, it is not sent to the server with every request.

You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Rejecting deletes the analytics cookies. You can also delete local storage and block cookies for any website in your browser settings:
