verified_user Do I have to accept cookies?

No. This website sets no cookies, so you won't see a cookie banner.

It only stores the language you choose, and only once you pick one. That is technical storage that identifies no one, so under European rules (Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC) and Swiss rules (Art. 45c of the Telecommunications Act) it does not need your consent — you only need to be informed, which is what this page does.

01 // What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar browser technology for saving small settings on your device; unlike cookies, it is not sent to the server with every request.

03 // External services the site connects to

These services do not set cookies through this website, but they receive your IP address when your browser connects to them (needed to send you the content):
