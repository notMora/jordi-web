A website with an online booking system, set up for you

A website with an online booking system lets your customers pick a time and book themselves, without calling or messaging you first. I set it up for SMEs across Switzerland that work by appointment: a booking calendar on your website, automatic confirmation by email or SMS, and optional card payment when booking. You work directly with me, remotely, in English, German or Spanish. The first step is a free 20-minute call.

01 — The problem

Still taking bookings by phone and WhatsApp?

  • You're with a customer and the phone rings again. Someone wants an appointment, so you either interrupt what you're doing or call back later, and by then they may already have booked somewhere else.
  • Some customers want to book in the evening or at the weekend, when you're closed. If the only way to get an appointment is to call during opening hours, not all of them will wait until tomorrow.
  • Bookings arrive by phone, WhatsApp, email and in person, and end up in a paper diary or in your head. Keeping track takes effort, and one slip is enough for a double booking or a forgotten appointment.
  • Someone books a slot and simply doesn't turn up. The time is blocked, you can't give it to anyone else, and nothing in your current process makes customers think twice before missing an appointment.
02 — What's included

What your online booking system includes

check

Booking calendar on your website

Customers see the times you've made available and book the slot that suits them, directly on your website. They can do it at any hour, and you no longer have to answer a call or message for every single booking.

check

Automatic confirmation by email or SMS

As soon as someone books, they automatically receive a confirmation by email or SMS with the details of their appointment. You don't have to confirm anything by hand, and your customer has everything in writing.

check

Optional card payment when booking

If you want, customers can pay by card when they book, for example a deposit. It's entirely optional and you decide whether to use it. Paying something upfront can make a booking feel more binding for your customer.

check

Part of your own website

The calendar sits on your own website, right next to your services, prices and contact details. Everything a customer needs to decide and book is in one place, within reach of the pages they're already reading.

check

Easy to book on a phone

Booking is part of a website designed for phones first. Choosing a time and confirming it is designed to be simple on a small screen, with buttons that are easy to tap with a thumb.

check

Set up and handed over for you

You don't have to configure any software yourself. I set up the booking system, test it on real devices and hand it over live, explaining in plain language how it works. Maintenance afterwards is optional.
