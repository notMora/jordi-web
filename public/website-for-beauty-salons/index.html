01 — The problem

Where a beauty salon loses time

  • A facial takes an hour or more, and you can't stop halfway through to answer the phone. Clients who want to book can't reach you, and when the treatment is over, a list of missed calls is waiting.
  • Your treatment list lives in a PDF, on Instagram or only in your head. Clients can't see what a treatment involves, how long it takes or what it costs, so they message you to ask before they book.
  • A two-hour slot for an expensive treatment is a real loss when the client doesn't turn up. Without a firm booking, and without the option to pay in advance, you carry that risk on your own.
  • Before a first treatment, clients have questions: can I come wearing make-up, how should I prepare, how long do lash extensions last? Every week you type the same answers into messages, often late in the evening.
02 — What's included

What a website design for beauty salons includes

check

A treatment menu with durations

Every treatment on one clear list: what it involves, how long it takes and what it costs, grouped the way clients think, for example face, body, nails and lashes. Clients understand what they're booking before they book it.

check

Online booking for longer treatments

Clients choose the treatment first, then a day and time that fit its length. They get a confirmation by email or SMS, and the booking appears in your calendar without you taking a single call.

check

Consultations before a treatment

For treatments that need a conversation first, clients can book a separate consultation instead of the treatment itself. You meet them, talk about what suits them, and then plan the treatment together with the time it really needs.

check

Optional card payment when booking

For costly or long treatments, clients can pay by card when they book. It's optional: you decide whether to use it at all. On the call, we look at where it makes sense for your salon and where it doesn't.

check

Nails and lashes with their own sections

Nail and lash services get their own space, with the details clients look for: techniques, infills, how long a new set takes. Useful if you run a nail studio or offer lashes alongside cosmetic treatments.

check

An assistant for pre-treatment questions

The AI assistant answers common questions 24/7 using your salon's information: how to prepare, what to avoid beforehand, how long a treatment takes. It gives no medical advice. When someone wants to book, it saves their details and lets you know.
