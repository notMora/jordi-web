01 — The problem

What gets in the way at a busy salon

  • Your hands are full of foils and colour when the phone rings. Short appointments mean lots of bookings, and every booking taken by phone interrupts the client sitting in your chair.
  • Clients want to book with their stylist, not just any free slot. When they can't see who is available and when, they call or message to ask, and the back-and-forth takes longer than the booking itself.
  • Prices depend on the service and on hair length, and many clients don't know what to expect. Without a clear price list online, they ask before booking or get a surprise at the till.
  • You're listed on a booking platform, but people looking for your salon land on a profile that looks like everyone else's. Your style, your team and your walk-in times don't come across.
02 — What's included

What's included in a website for hair salons and barbershops

check

Booking with a specific stylist

Clients choose the service, then the person they want, then a time when that person is free. They get a confirmation by email or SMS. Regulars book their usual stylist in a few taps, without having to call.

check

Prices by service and hair length

Cut, colour, highlights, blow-dry, beard trim: each service with its price, and price ranges by hair length where that applies. Clients know roughly what to expect before they sit down, and fewer of them need to ask.

check

Walk-in times for barbershops

If you take walk-ins, the website says so clearly: which days, which hours, and when it's better to book instead. Clients who don't want to wait can still reserve a slot online from their phone.

check

Works alongside your salon software

If you already use salon software or a booking platform, the website doesn't replace it. We decide together whether clients book through the website's own calendar or through the system you already have, in which case the site links straight to it.

check

Your team and your style

A short introduction to each stylist or barber, with their specialities and photos of their work. Regulars find their person, and new clients get a feel for your salon before they walk in for the first time.

check

An assistant for quick questions

The AI assistant answers common questions 24/7 from your salon's information: opening hours, prices, walk-in times, parking. When someone wants an appointment, it saves their details and lets you know, and it follows up if they don't reply.
