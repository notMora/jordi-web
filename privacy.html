03 // What data I process and why

A. Contact form

Data: name, email, phone/WhatsApp and message; website or Instagram if you provide it.

Purpose: to answer your enquiry and, if you ask for one, prepare a quote.

Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for general enquiries, my legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The form's checkbox only confirms that you have read this policy.

B. Call booking

Data: name, email, phone/WhatsApp, the service you are interested in, chosen date and time, time zone, site language and optional notes.

Purpose: to schedule the free 20-minute call, send you the confirmation (emailed by Cal.com) and prepare for it.

Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

C. Clients

Data: contact and billing details, project communications and materials.

Purpose: to deliver the contracted service, invoice and meet tax obligations.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

D. Browsing the website

Data: IP address, date and time, page visited and browser type.

Purpose: the hosting server logs this data for security and operation. In addition, when you scroll to the booking section your browser connects to Cal.com (to show available times), which receives your IP address in order to respond.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

E. Language preference

Data: the lang key in your browser's local storage (en, es or de). It contains no personal data and never leaves your device.

Purpose: to show the site in the language you chose on your next visit. It is only saved when you pick a language. More details in the Cookie Policy.

F. Website analytics (only with your consent)

Data: pseudonymous identifiers stored in cookies (_ga), pages visited, referring page, device and browser type, approximate location (country/city) derived from the IP address, and actions such as sending the form, booking a call or tapping WhatsApp or email. Google Analytics 4 does not store your IP address.

Purpose: to see which pages are visited and which ones lead to enquiries, so I can improve the website. It only runs if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner; without your consent it is not loaded.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Art. 45c of the Swiss Telecommunications Act). You can withdraw it at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Fields marked with * in the forms are needed to reply to you or book the call. I do not make automated decisions or build profiles with your data.

04 // How long I keep it

  • Enquiries and bookings that do not lead to a contract: up to 12 months from the last contact, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Clients: for the duration of the project and afterwards for the periods required by applicable tax and commercial law.
  • Server logs: for the period set by the hosting provider for security purposes.
  • Analytics (only with consent): user-level data is deleted by Google Analytics after 2 months; the cookies last up to 2 years unless you reject or delete them.

WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta): if you tap those links you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply.

06 // International transfers

I run my business from Ecuador, so the data you send me is processed there. Ecuador does not have an adequacy decision from the European Commission or Switzerland; this transfer is necessary to handle your request or the contract you ask for, and I apply the safeguards described in this policy.

Some providers process data in the USA. Cal.com states that it relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its Swiss-US extension. For the others, please see their privacy policies linked above.

Para qué: mostrarte la web en el idioma que elegiste en tu próxima visita. Solo se guarda cuando eliges un idioma. Más detalle en la Política de Cookies.

Los campos marcados con * en los formularios son necesarios para poder responderte o agendar la llamada. No tomo decisiones automatizadas ni elaboro perfiles con tus datos.
